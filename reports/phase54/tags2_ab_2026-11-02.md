**No rule passes the bar with either history** (0 of 55 on both candidate sets).
On `tags` candidates, 15 rules are positive in both halves with tags-1 and 10
with tags-2, all with fewer than 10 round trips.

## Reading

- tags-2 fixes what the audit measured (moods, sarcasm, macro side), and it
  moves single picks, but one month of history with 4 to 6 round trips per rule
  cannot tell better tags from luck: the largest swing is one trade.
- The rule-level evidence says the AI direction term adds nothing over plain
  snap-back on the tag-picked coins. The tag pick itself, which uses mentions,
  not AI direction, is the part that has helped (+8.18% against −42.92% for the
  liquid 20).
- No live change follows from this. Round 2 (around 11-08) judges on data after
  2026-11-01T02Z, all tags-2.
