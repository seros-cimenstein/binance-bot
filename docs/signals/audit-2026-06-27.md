## Detector Semantics

The `confidence` fields are not comparable across sources.

| Source | What emits a signal | What confidence means |
|---|---:|---|
| DexScreener | New token profile, then best qualifying pair with age <= 24h, liquidity >= $5k, 24h volume >= $10k | Liquidity strength above $5k multiplied by volume scaling to $100k |
| Smart-money | Helius wallet transaction with incoming non-stable SPL token transfer into a watched wallet and no outgoing transfer of same mint | Watched wallet weight |
| Telegram | Regex contract address from configured Telegram channels | Configured channel weight |

Telegram is configured with placeholder public/news channels: `binance_announcements`, `WatcherGuru`, and `coingecko`. Those channels are not expected to post raw contracts often enough for this parser to be useful.

## 4. Would We Have Made Money?

Current survivor data gives ground-truth corroboration for the warning below, not for profitability: the huge detection-time `h1`/`h6`/`h24` `priceChange` fields mostly did not persist. Among live DexScreener pairs, median current price is 90.7% below detection, and 81.3% of live price-comparable pairs are down at least 25%. This still is not a realized PnL replay, because it lacks the post-detection price path and any possible exit peaks.

No realized PnL exists. A real answer still needs post-detection price snapshots and executable fill assumptions. The current survivor data is useful ground truth, but it is a stale current-state endpoint, not a trade replay.

Friction reality with a $15.63 wallet:

| Venue / chain | Assumed round-trip fixed cost | Fixed cost as % of $15.63 | Plus 2% in + 2% out slippage | Break-even move before spread/MEV |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Solana meme trade | $0.50 | 3.2% | 4.0% | > 7.2% |
| Ethereum DEX trade | $5.00 | 32.0% | 4.0% | > 36.0% |

This is optimistic because the capital is in a Binance USDT wallet, while these signals are mostly DEX/on-chain assets. Funding, bridging, withdrawals, gas inventory, failed transactions, and stale quotes are not included.

If one incorrectly treats the detection-time `priceChange` window as if it were a post-detection peak, the numbers look attractive. That is not a valid PnL replay. It mostly says "these tokens had already moved."

## 5. Source Verdicts

### DexScreener

Verdict: keep as a monitored candidate source, but do not execute from it yet.

Why:

- It produced stable volume: 87-105/day on full recent days.
- It has real liquidity/volume filters.
- It records enough detection-time market data to support better replay later.
- But the current survivor outcome is poor: 45.3% hit the rug proxy and 75.3% are catastrophic by the audit definition.
- It still has no realized fills or post-detection price path snapshots.
- Many signals are already post-move at detection.

Rejected paper filter:

- Solana only.
- confidence >= 0.40.
- pair age <= 120 minutes.

That filter produced 43 Solana entries over this sample, but survivor data was bad: median current price change -99.0%, 33 rug proxies, and 39 catastrophic outcomes. The next paper test should record post-detection snapshots first, then search for a better filter instead of assuming the current confidence score is enough.

### Smart-money

Verdict: keep only as an enrichment source until it records price/liquidity snapshots.

Why:

- 169 alerts from 6 firing wallets out of the bootstrapped wallet set.
- 131 unique tokens, with a 22.5% re-fire rate.
- Every confidence is 0.6, so confidence is not a ranking tool.
- Only 2 smart-money tokens overlapped with DexScreener detections.
- The ledger cannot answer whether these wallets bought before the move, after the move, or into illiquid tokens.
- Detector lag is usually under a minute, but 16 alerts were more than 1 hour after block time.

This source may become useful if each alert is joined immediately to DexScreener/Birdeye price, liquidity, FDV, and pair age. In its current ledger form, it is not tradeable evidence.

### Telegram

Verdict: shut down or replace the channel list.

Why:

- It produced 0 entries in six-plus days.
- The configured channels are placeholder public/news channels.
- The ingester only emits contract addresses found by regex.
- Public announcement/news channels usually do not post fresh raw token contracts in the format this parser expects.

Recommendation: either curate real alpha/private channels and keep the ingester, or remove this source from the signal stack. The current configuration adds no signal.

## 6. Honest Conclusion

This is not tradeable today.

The new survivor data strengthens that recommendation rather than relaxing it. DexScreener produced 607 signals, but 45.3% now hit the rug proxy and 75.3% are catastrophic by the audit definition. The only large chain sample, Solana, has a median live-pair price change of -92.5% from detection, and the previously suggested Solana confidence/age filter is worse: median -99.0% with 39 of 43 catastrophic. Smart-money also looks weak as a standalone source: only 15 of 131 unique tokens still had a same-chain pair with at least $1k liquidity a median 3.8 days later. This does not fabricate PnL, but it is enough current-state evidence to reject execution.

Reasons:

- The survivor analysis is now complete and shows a high rug/catastrophic rate plus severe current price decay, especially on Solana.
- The wallet is about $15.63. Even if Solana round-trip fixed cost is only $0.50, that is 3.2% of capital before spread and slippage. Ethereum-sized fixed fees are completely impractical at this account size.
- DexScreener signals are plentiful, but many appear after large backward-looking moves. Without post-detection snapshots, volume is not edge.
- Smart-money does not log price/liquidity at detection, so it cannot support even a rough PnL replay.
- Telegram produced nothing because the channel list is placeholder quality.

Recommendation: run a longer paper-replay first, not execution.

The most efficient next test:

1. Keep DexScreener as monitored-only; do not execute the current confidence/age filter.
2. For every Solana candidate, snapshot price, liquidity, volume, FDV, pair existence, and detection-time momentum at detection, +5m, +15m, +1h, +6h, and +24h.
3. Search for a new filter only after scoring net returns after at least 7.2% round-trip friction for the current $15.63 wallet size.
4. Separately enrich smart-money alerts with the same market snapshot fields before judging them.
5. Disable Telegram until real channels are curated.

Execution should wait until this replay shows that a filtered source survives liquidity/rug checks and clears friction after detection, not just before detection.
